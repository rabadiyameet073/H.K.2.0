What is a Polyhouse?

A polyhouse is a cost-effective greenhouse variant sheathed in UV-stabilized polyethylene film over a galvanized iron (GI) frame. Extensively adopted across Gujarat, Maharashtra, and North India with up to 75% subsidy support.

Can Protected Farming
Be 100% Organic?

  • Yes. Protected cultivation and certified organic methods are natural commercial partners.
  • Biological Pest Control: Insect-proof netting, yellow/blue sticky traps, and neem bio-extracts prevent chemical sprays.
  • Organic Nutrition: Micro-dosing vermicompost slurry, Jeevamrut, and enriched bio-fertilizers via drip.
  • Accredited Certification: Compliant with NPOP (National Programme for Organic Production) & PGS-India.
  • Zero Residue Advantage: Captures commanding 30%–50% price premiums in Tier-1 metros and export markets.

• Yield: 25–30 tons / acre
• Realized Price: ₹10–₹15 / kg (Seasonal Glut)

• Key Advantage: Vertical growth, blemish-free grade, zero rain damage

The 5 Big Levers for Farm Profitability

Actionable strategic levers to maximize net return per acre without expanding landholding size
Lever 1
High-Value Crop Economics
Shift acreage from low-margin bulk commodities (cotton/castor) to spices, seed crops, and high-margin protected vegetables.
Lever 2
Disintermediate Supply Chain
Bypass 3–4 middlemen through FPOs, farmer cooperatives, supermarket contracts, and direct urban farmers' markets.
Lever 3
On-Farm Value Addition
Participate in downstream value: Cleaned wheat ➔ Packaged Atta (₹60/kg); Groundnut ➔ Roasted snack packs (₹200/kg).
Lever 4
Diversified Revenue Streams
Insulate cash flow: Cash crop + Polyhouse horticulture + Dairy integration + Vermicompost packaging + Nursery seedlings.
Lever 5
Input Cost Rationalization
Eliminate ₹80,000 poison bill with on-farm Jeevamrut; prevent water waste with drip; buy inputs collectively in FPO.
